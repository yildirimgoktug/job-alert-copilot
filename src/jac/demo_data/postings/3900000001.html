AI Research Intern (part-time)

Northwind Data Lab

Northwind Data Lab is a small research team in Amsterdam that builds retrieval and language-model tools for the public sector. We are looking for a student to join us as an AI research intern.

What you will do

  • Evaluate retrieval-augmented generation pipelines and report the results to the team.
  • Write clean Python and PyTorch code and test it with pytest.
  • Work together with two senior engineers on an internal search tool.

What we ask

  • You are studying for a Bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science or a related field.
  • You are fluent in English. Dutch is a plus, but it is not required.
  • Experience with Python and machine learning from your courses or projects is enough.

This is a part-time position of 16 hours per week during the semester. The office is in Amsterdam and you can work from home two days a week.
